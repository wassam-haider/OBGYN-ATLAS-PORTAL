1. EARLY PREGNANCY

Condition / Test Normal / reassuring Abnormal Diagnostic / important cut-off
Quantitative β-hCG Gestation-dependent Abnormal rise/fall No single diagnostic value for ectopic
48-h β-hCG Expected rise depends on starting value Suboptimal rise/plateau/fall Interpret with TVUS + clinical findings
Serum progesterone in PUL — — Not recommended routinely to determine location/viability
TVUS gestational sac Intrauterine sac with appropriate features Empty/abnormal sac Diagnostic criteria require appropriate measurements + repeat scan where necessary

Ectopic pregnancy — remember

TVUS + quantitative β-hCG

No β-hCG "discriminatory zone" should be used alone to diagnose ectopic pregnancy.

SECTION 41

42. TAPS

IOC: MCA-PSV in both twins

Suspicion:

One twin anaemic + other polycythaemic.

The exact diagnostic staging uses intertwin MCA-PSV differences and gestational-age-adjusted MoM values, rather than one universal absolute velocity.

SECTION 42

Characteristic:

If the value is gestational-age dependent, assay dependent, MoM based, centile based, or clinical-diagnosis based, do not invent a universal numerical cut-off.

One important terminology rule

For your final MRCOG/FCPS notes, I recommend using:

FLI = First-line investigation

IOC = Investigation of choice

GS = Gold standard/definitive test

and writing “No true gold standard” whenever the diagnosis is fundamentally clinical—for example placental abruption, shoulder dystocia, cord prolapse, chorioamnionitis and postpartum endometritis. This prevents the common exam error of calling an ultrasound or blood test a “gold standard” when it actually is not.

Combine all

MASTER RCOG/NICE OBSTETRICS

➔ Conditions → First-Line Investigation → Investigation of Choice → Gold Standard → Normal → Abnormal → Diagnostic Cut-off → Severe/Critical Cut-off → Interpretation

Exam rule: First-line ≠ gold standard. Where no true gold-standard test exists, it is explicitly stated. Numerical values should be interpreted with gestational age, laboratory reference ranges, MoM/centiles, assay and clinical context.

Diagnostic Cut-offs Matrix

Core associations, key diagnostic parameters, and rapid exam differentiation:

📌 1. EARLY PREGNANCY
  • ● Quantitative β-hCG: Gestation-dependent
  • ● 48-h β-hCG: Expected rise depends on starting value
  • ● Serum progesterone in PUL: —
  • ● TVUS gestational sac: Intrauterine sac with appropriate features
  • ● CRL: Gestation-dependent
  • ● Mean sac diameter: Gestation-dependent
📌 2. RECURRENT MISCARRIAGE
  • ● Investigation: Normal
  • ● Lupus anticoagulant: Negative
  • ● Anticardiolipin IgG/IgM: Negative/low
  • ● Anti-β2 glycoprotein-I: Negative
  • ● APS confirmation: —
  • ● Uterine assessment: Normal anatomy
📌 3. HYPEREMESIS GRAVIDARUM
  • ● Parameter: Normal
  • ● Sodium: ~135–145 mmol/L
  • ● Potassium: ~3.5–5.0 mmol/L
  • ● Creatinine: Pregnancy-specific
  • ● Urea: Pregnancy-specific
  • ● Urine ketones: Negative
📌 4. FETAL GROWTH RESTRICTION
  • ● EFW / AC: Interpretation
  • ● ≥10th centile: Not SGA by conventional definition
  • ● <10th centile: SGA
  • ● <3rd centile: Severe SGA
  • ● Finding: Interpretation
  • ● Normal forward EDF: Reassuring
📌 5. FETAL ANAEMIA
  • ● MCA-PSV: Interpretation
  • ● <1.5 MoM: Generally below intervention threshold
  • ● ≥1.5 MoM: Significant fetal anaemia suspected
📌 6. AMNIOTIC FLUID
  • ● DVP: Interpretation
  • ● <2 cm: Oligohydramnios
  • ● 2–8 cm: Generally normal
  • ● >8 cm: Polyhydramnios
  • ● AFI: Interpretation
  • ● <5 cm: Oligohydramnios
📌 7. CERVICAL LENGTH
  • ● Cervical length: Interpretation
  • ● >25 mm: Not short
  • ● ≤25 mm: Short cervix
  • ● ≤15 mm: Very short / substantially increased risk
📌 8. FETAL FIBRONECTIN
  • ● fFN: Interpretation
  • ● <50 ng/mL: Negative/reassuring
  • ● ≥50 ng/mL: Positive
📌 9. PPROM
  • ● Finding: Interpretation
  • ● No pooling: Does not completely exclude ROM
  • ● Visible pooling: Strong evidence
  • ● PAMG-1 positive: Supports ROM
  • ● IGFBP-1 positive: Supports ROM
📌 10. HYPERTENSION IN PREGNANCY
  • ● BP: Interpretation
  • ● <140/90 mmHg: Below hypertension threshold
  • ● ≥140/90: Hypertension
  • ● ≥160 systolic OR ≥110 diastolic: Severe hypertension
📌 11. PRE-ECLAMPSIA
  • ● Test: Normal / below threshold
  • ● PCR: <30 mg/mmol
  • ● ACR: <8 mg/mmol
  • ● Creatinine: Interpretation
  • ● <90 µmol/L: Below NICE renal dysfunction threshold
  • ● ≥90 µmol/L: Renal dysfunction in pre-eclampsia definition
📌 12. HELLP
  • ● Component: Important abnormality
  • ● Haemolysis: ↑LDH, ↑bilirubin, ↓haptoglobin, schistocytes
  • ● Elevated liver enzymes: ↑AST/ALT
  • ● Low platelets: <100 ×10⁹/L
