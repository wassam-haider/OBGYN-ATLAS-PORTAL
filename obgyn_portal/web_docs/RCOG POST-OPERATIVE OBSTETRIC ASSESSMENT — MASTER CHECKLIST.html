VTE assessment

Every postpartum/postoperative woman requires VTE risk assessment.

Ask/examine for:

Unilateral leg swelling

Calf pain

Tenderness

Warmth

Redness

PE

Sudden dyspnoea

Chest pain

Haemoptysis

Tachycardia

Hypoxia

Collapse

RCOG recommends postpartum VTE risk assessment and appropriate LMWH prophylaxis. For example, its Green-top Guideline 37a states that after caesarean birth, all women should be considered for 10 days of LMWH, with longer prophylaxis for women at high risk; persistent additional risk factors may require extension.

RCOG recommends postpartum risk assessment at least once after delivery and before discharge; its current Green-top 37a recommendations include 10 days of LMWH for intermediate-risk women and 6 weeks for high-risk women, with extension when persistent risk factors remain.
