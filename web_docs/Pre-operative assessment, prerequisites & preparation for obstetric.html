Pre-operative assessment, prerequisites & preparation for obstetric procedures — RCOG/MRCOG framework

⭐ This is a practical MRCOG/obstetric theatre checklist covering the major obstetric procedures you may encounter in labour ward, emergency theatre, elective theatre and postpartum haemorrhage (PPH). RCOG guidance is procedure-specific rather than one single universal “pre-op checklist”; the framework below synthesises the RCOG curriculum, consent guidance and relevant Green-top Guidelines. RCOG specifically expects knowledge of operative vaginal birth, breech/twin delivery, caesarean section, perineal trauma, retained placenta, anaesthesia, resuscitation and obstetric emergencies.

SECTION 26

20. THE “10-POINT MRCOG PRE-OP” ANSWER

If the examiner asks:

“How would you prepare this patient for the procedure?”

Give this structure:

1

Indication

“First, I would confirm the indication and urgency.”

2

Maternal assessment

“I would perform ABCDE assessment and assess comorbidities and haemodynamic stability.”

3

Fetal assessment

“I would assess fetal wellbeing, presentation, position, gestation and estimated weight as appropriate.”

4

Investigations

“I would obtain appropriate blood tests including FBC and group-and-save/crossmatch depending on haemorrhage risk.”
