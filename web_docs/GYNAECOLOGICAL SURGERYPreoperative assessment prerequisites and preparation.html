O

BOWEL PREPARATION

Routine bowel preparation is NOT required for most gynaecological operations.

Consider selectively when:

Significant bowel surgery anticipated

Deep infiltrating endometriosis with bowel involvement

Colorectal involvement

Selected oncology procedures

Follow the colorectal/gynaecology multidisciplinary plan.

P

VAGINAL PREPARATION

Before major vaginal/pelvic surgery:

Appropriate hygiene

Avoid unnecessary shaving

Antiseptic preparation according to theatre protocol

Appropriate bladder catheterisation
