SECTION 1

Overview & Core Guidance

An MDT (multidisciplinary team) is particularly important when an O&G condition involves major haemorrhage, maternal organ disease, malignancy, complex pelvic surgery, fetal/neonatal risk, or another specialty beyond routine O&G care. RCOG's current framework includes four major O&G subspecialties: Maternal & Fetal Medicine, Gynaecological Oncology, Reproductive Medicine and Urogynaecology.

SECTION 2

I. OBSTETRIC CONDITIONS REQUIRING MDT

A

Major haemorrhage / placental disorders

Condition Important MDT members
Placenta accreta spectrum (accreta/increta/percreta) ⭐⭐⭐ Consultant obstetrician/MFM, anaesthetist, pelvic surgeon/gynae-oncologist, urologist, interventional radiology where appropriate, haematology/transfusion, neonatology, ICU
Placenta previa with previous CS / suspected PAS MFM, senior obstetrician, anaesthesia, blood bank/transfusion, pelvic surgeon
Placenta percreta with bladder invasion O&G + urology + anaesthesia + transfusion + ICU ± gynae-oncology
Major antepartum haemorrhage Senior O&G, anaesthesia, haematology/transfusion, neonatology ± IR
Massive PPH / refractory PPH Senior O&G, anaesthesia, haematology/transfusion, theatre team, ICU ± IR/general surgery
Uterine rupture Senior obstetrician, anaesthetist, neonatal team, blood bank ± urology/general surgery
Morbidly adherent placenta with suspected pelvic invasion Specialist pelvic surgical MDT

High-yield: PAS is one of the clearest examples of an obstetric MDT condition. The updated RCOG Green-top Guideline 27a specifically recommends an antenatal care plan involving an expert MDT and delivery in a specialist centre for women with a high probability of PAS.

SECTION 25

🔑 MDT EXAM RULE

When deciding whether MDT is needed, ask:

Does the condition involve >1 organ system, major haemorrhage, malignancy, major fetal abnormality, severe maternal disease, complex surgery, or specialist treatment?

➔ If yes → specialist MDT should be considered/activated.

For example:

➔ Placenta percreta → O&G/MFM + anaesthesia + blood bank/transfusion + urology + pelvic surgeon ± IR + ICU + neonatology.
