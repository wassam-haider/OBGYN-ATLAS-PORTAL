SECTION 21

20. ECTOPIC PREGNANCY — NON-OPERATIVE

Expectant management

Follow:

➔ β-hCG every few days → until appropriately declining/negative according to pathway.

Written instructions

RCOG specifically states that women managed expectantly should have access to a direct hospital contact number and return immediately if symptoms occur.

SECTION 22

21. ECTOPIC — METHOTREXATE

Follow-up is mandatory

Typical monitoring:

➔ Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 7 β-hCG

Then:

weekly β-hCG until negative/appropriately resolved according to protocol.

Important

Day 4 rise can occur.

➔ Compare Day 4 → Day 7.
➔ Inadequate decline → further treatment/reassessment.

Persistent symptoms can indicate rupture despite falling β-hCG.

Avoid

Pregnancy

Folic acid supplementation if instructed by protocol

Alcohol according to local methotrexate protocol

NSAIDs/other interacting drugs where relevant

Sexual intercourse/strenuous activity according to local advice

Emergency symptoms

Increasing abdominal pain

Shoulder-tip pain

Collapse

Dizziness

Heavy bleeding

SECTION 24

23. MEDICAL MANAGEMENT OF MISCARRIAGE

After misoprostol:

Patient advice

Expect:

Heavy bleeding

RCOG states that if bleeding has not started 24 hours after treatment, the patient should contact the early pregnancy service/hospital. Pregnancy testing is generally performed around 3 weeks after treatment, with further assessment if positive.

SECTION 55

53. DISCHARGE FOLLOW-UP BY TIME

A useful MRCOG/clinical exam framework:

Time What to review
Same day Stability, diagnosis, treatment, safety-net
24–72 h Infection, pain, acute complications, PID/ectopic protocols where applicable
1 week Wound, symptoms, histology if available, treatment response
2 weeks Surgical recovery, miscarriage/expectant pathways, persistent symptoms
4–6 weeks Major postoperative recovery, hysterectomy, prolapse/incontinence, pathology
6–12 weeks Functional recovery, pelvic floor, sexual function, long-term plan
3–6 months Chronic disease, endometriosis, fibroids, prolapse, recurrent symptoms
Long-term Cancer surveillance, recurrent disease, menopause, chronic pelvic pain
Important
these are practical intervals, not universal RCOG-mandated timings. The exact timing should be determined by the condition, procedure, pathology and local pathway.
