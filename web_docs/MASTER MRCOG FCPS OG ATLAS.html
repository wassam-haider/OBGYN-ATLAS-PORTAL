I’ll keep this entirely text-based, with no pictures, and expand the material into a revision-atlas format rather than leaving topics as one-line facts.

SECTION 61

PART LVIII — HIGH-YIELD "WHY?" QUESTIONS

Question Core explanation
Why is the ureter vulnerable during hysterectomy? It lies close to the uterine artery and cervix/cardinal ligament region.
Why can cervical cancer spread to pelvic nodes? Cervical lymphatics drain through parametrial and pelvic nodal pathways.
Why can fundal uterine disease involve inguinal nodes? Lymphatics can accompany the round ligament.
Why does ovarian cancer involve para-aortic nodes? Ovarian embryology and lymphatic drainage relate to its original posterior abdominal location.
Why does endometriosis cause pain? Inflammation, haemorrhage, fibrosis, adhesions and altered nociception contribute.
Why does PCOS cause anovulation? Abnormal follicular development with androgenic, gonadotrophin and metabolic signalling prevents normal dominant follicle maturation.
Why does pregnancy cause anaemia? Plasma volume rises more than RBC mass.
Why does creatinine fall in pregnancy? GFR and renal blood flow increase.
Why does PaCO₂ fall? Progesterone increases maternal ventilation.
Why does breastfeeding suppress ovulation? Prolactin suppresses hypothalamic GnRH/LH pulsatility.
Why does brain sparing occur? Increased placental resistance causes fetal redistribution toward vital organs.
Why does shoulder dystocia occur? The shoulders fail to complete normal rotation/descent, often with anterior shoulder impaction behind the symphysis.
Why does PAS cause major haemorrhage? Abnormal placental adherence/invasion prevents normal separation and effective uterine haemostasis.
