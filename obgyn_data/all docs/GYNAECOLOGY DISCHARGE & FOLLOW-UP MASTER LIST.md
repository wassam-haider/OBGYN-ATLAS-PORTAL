Every discharge should answer:

What is the diagnosis? What has been done? What happens next? When will she be reviewed? What should make her return urgently?

53. DISCHARGE FOLLOW-UP BY TIME

A useful MRCOG/clinical exam framework:

Time	What to review
Same day	Stability, diagnosis, treatment, safety-net
24–72 h	Infection, pain, acute complications, PID/ectopic protocols where applicable
1 week	Wound, symptoms, histology if available, treatment response
2 weeks	Surgical recovery, miscarriage/expectant pathways, persistent symptoms
4–6 weeks	Major postoperative recovery, hysterectomy, prolapse/incontinence, pathology
6–12 weeks	Functional recovery, pelvic floor, sexual function, long-term plan
3–6 months	Chronic disease, endometriosis, fibroids, prolapse, recurrent symptoms
Long-term	Cancer surveillance, recurrent disease, menopause, chronic pelvic pain

Important: these are practical intervals, not universal RCOG-mandated timings. The exact timing should be determined by the condition, procedure, pathology and local pathway.
