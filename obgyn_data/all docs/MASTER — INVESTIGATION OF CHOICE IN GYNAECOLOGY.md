🚨 HIGH-YIELD EXAM CORRECTIONS

1. HMB:
❌ “ET ≥4.5 mm = biopsy”
✅ No universal NICE 4.5-mm HMB cutoff.

2. Endometriosis:
❌ “Laparoscopy is always first-line.”
✅ TVUS first-line; laparoscopy when appropriate.

3. PCOS:
❌ “Ultrasound is mandatory.”
✅ 2/3 Rotterdam criteria; ultrasound may not be needed.

4. POI:
❌ “FSH >30 twice is the NICE definition.”
✅ Elevated FSH twice, 4–6 weeks apart + clinical picture.

5. PID:
❌ “NAAT diagnoses PID.”
✅ PID is primarily a clinical diagnosis; NAAT supports aetiology.

6. BV:
❌ “HVS culture is gold standard.”
✅ Amsel/Nugent/Hay-Ison are appropriate diagnostic approaches.

7. SUI:
❌ “Urodynamics is first-line.”
✅ Clinical diagnosis; urodynamics only in selected situations.

8. Infertility:
❌ “Everyone needs laparoscopy + dye.”
✅ HSG/HyCoSy first in low-risk women; laparoscopy when pelvic disease is suspected.

9. Ovarian cancer:
❌ “CA-125 >35 = ovarian cancer.”
✅ CA-125 is a risk/investigation marker, not a cancer diagnosis.

10. Cervical screening:
❌ Cytology as the primary UK screening test.
✅ Primary HPV screening.
