## 📋 Source Categories Covered
1. **Obstetrics & Delivery:** Emergency algorithms, CTG interpretation, PAS, PPH, shoulder dystocia, operative delivery.
2. **Gynecology & Oncology:** Menstrual disorders, pelvic masses, colposcopy, staging, reproductive endocrinology.
3. **OSCE & TOACS Station Guides:** Station frameworks, candidate scripts, ICE models, patient communication rubrics.
4. **Laboratory & Radiology Atlases:** Biochemical cut-offs, ultrasound diagnostic criteria, MRI/CT signs.
